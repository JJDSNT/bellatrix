# AI_context/consolidated

## Promotion rule

No issue may be moved to consolidated without:

- implementation complete
- documentation updated
- acceptance criteria satisfied

## Purpose

Turn finished work into permanent knowledge.
Session investigations that produced durable knowledge also live here,
named after their subject.

## Layout

- `history/` — closed issues, moved here from `../issues/` with
  `status: consolidated`. The record of what was done and why.
- everything else — knowledge documents named after their subject, in the
  `templates/consolidated.template.md` shape.

A closed issue lands in `history/` even when its outcome is already written up
in `docs/`. The document says what is true now; the issue says how it was
found, what was decided along the way, and what was deliberately left undone.
